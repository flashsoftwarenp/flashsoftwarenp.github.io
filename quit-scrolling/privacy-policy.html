Privacy Policy for Quit Scrolling

This privacy policy applies to the Quit Scrolling mobile app (the "Application") for Android, published by Flash Software (the "Service Provider").

Quit Scrolling helps you set your own limits on how much you scroll in apps you choose. It runs entirely on your device.


Summary

Quit Scrolling does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign in, and no server. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it is not technically able to send your information anywhere. Everything the app records stays in the app's private storage on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall it.


Use of the Accessibility Service

Quit Scrolling uses Android's AccessibilityService API. This is the only way an Android app can tell that you are scrolling in another app, and it is what makes the app's core features possible.

You are shown a disclosure screen inside the app explaining this, and you must accept it before the app will take you to Android's settings to switch the service on. Nothing is counted until you enable it yourself.

While the service is enabled:

  • What the app receives. Android notifies the app which application is currently in the foreground, and when a scroll or a content change occurs in it.
  • What the app does with it. The app acts only on the applications you have chosen to watch. For those, it counts scrolls and time in use so it can show your live counter, notify you as you approach a limit you set, and show your full screen reminder when you reach it.
  • What the app does not do. It does not read, store, or transmit the text, images, messages, contacts, passwords, or any other content shown on your screen. It does not record keystrokes or taps. It does not take screenshots. It does not use the accessibility service for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the features described above.

Data gathered through the accessibility service is never shared with the Service Provider or with any third party, because it never leaves your device.


Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, in its own private storage, so that it can show you your own statistics:

  • The list of applications you chose to watch, and the daily limit you set for each
  • A record of each time a watched application was opened, with the number of scrolls counted and how long it was in use
  • Your settings, such as reminder preferences, bedtime hours, focus sessions, and the position and style of the on screen counter

This information is not personal data held by the Service Provider. It is not uploaded, backed up to any service operated by the Service Provider, or accessible to anyone but you.


Information the Service Provider collects

None. The Service Provider does not collect your device identifiers, your IP address, your location, your contacts, crash reports, usage analytics, or any other information from the Application.


Third party services

The Application contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third party software development kits that collect data. It does not use Google Analytics, Firebase, or any comparable service.


Other permissions the app uses
  • Notifications. Used only to send you the reminders and daily summaries produced by the app itself. You may decline this permission, or turn the notifications off in the app, and everything else continues to work.
  • Query installed applications. Used to show you a list of the apps on your phone so you can pick which ones to watch. The list is read on your device and is never transmitted.
  • Battery optimisation exemption (optional). You may choose to exempt the app from battery optimisation so that Android does not stop the service while you are using a watched app. This is optional and grants no access to your data.

Children

The Application is not directed at children under 16. Because the Service Provider collects no data at all, no personal information is knowingly or unknowingly collected from children.


Data retention and deletion

Because nothing is transmitted, the Service Provider holds no data to retain or delete. The information stored on your device remains there until you delete it. You can clear a single app's counts from inside the app, clear the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstall Quit Scrolling, which removes everything it has saved.


Your rights

Data protection laws, including the GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act, give you rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. The Service Provider holds no personal data about you and does not sell or share any, so there is nothing to disclose or erase. If you have a question about this, you may contact the Service Provider at the address below.


Security

The information the app records is held in Android's private per app storage, which other applications cannot read. Keeping the data on your device, and never transmitting it, is the main protection this app offers.
